            Mastery Glyphs

            Shaping Glyphs cannot be combined with Basic Attack. Choose Use as Focus when the weapon is only part of the casting fiction.

            Basic Attack and Use as Focus are explicit, mutually exclusive weapon modes.

            Spells may combine multiple Source Glyphs. A Basic Attack can carry only one Source Glyph; that Source becomes Smite, consumes Mana, and adds no AP.

            Glyph metadata supplies localized names, grammatical forms, descriptions, and alternate incantation spellings.

            Mana or Dust adds +1 potential by default, but Glyph metadata may add bonuses or mode/target-specific overrides. Shaping Glyph delivery is layered and area resistance uses total selected Glyph count.

            Reference

            Combat Guide

            1 · Build

            Mastery Glyphs modify one attack

            Attack1 AP · 2 potential+ Power+1 AP · +1 potential+ Precision+1 AP · +1 potential= 3 AP · 4 potential

            Attack Mastery Glyphs are not separate attacks. Three Basic Attacks are three independent accuracy rolls and three independent damage chains.

            2 · Resolve

            Potential is a ceiling

            Accuracy vs DEXsuccess → woundHigher of Accuracy/Impact vs CONsuccess → woundHigher of Accuracy/Impact vs next defensetie/win → stop

            Each potential is one contest and at most one wound. The chain ends as soon as the defender wins or ties.

            3 · Apply

            Wounds wait for round end

            d10+2 pending→ d6

            Queue wounds during the round. Attribute reductions take effect only after End round, preventing early burst damage from weakening later defenses in the same round.

            Mastery Glyphs

            Quick reference

            Power
            +1 potential; accuracy die +1 tier.
            Precision
            +1 potential; advantage on accuracy; one attribute may be wounded twice.
            Dashing
            +1 potential; move potential × 5 m as part of the attack.
            Disarming
            1 AP · +1 potential. On a failed enemy Brace, the enemy drops a held item. The normal damage chain is unchanged.
            Knockback
            1 AP · +1 potential. On a failed enemy Brace, knock the target back Potential × 5 m. The normal damage chain is unchanged.
            Sweep
            Multiple targets; no extra potential.
            Important

            Table reminders

            • Ties favor the defender.
            • Losing a melee accuracy contest causes 1 chip damage.
            • Normally one attack cannot wound the same attribute twice.
            • From the second stage of the damage chain onward, resist whichever is higher: the accuracy roll or weapon Impact.
            • Spellcasting accuracy uses whichever is higher: INT or AUR.
            • The wounded attribute follows the injury fiction; it is not automatically the defense that failed.
            • Quick remains undefined and is intentionally omitted.
            • Shaping Glyphs cannot be combined with Basic Attack. Choose Use as Focus for weapon-assisted casting fiction.
            • Spells may combine many Source Glyphs. Basic Attack may carry only one, and its Smite costs Mana but no AP.
            • Precision + Lightning + Fire + Bolt = 4 AP while using the weapon as Focus or no weapon at all; Bolt contributes an extra +1 potential for focused application.
            • Glyph effects are working drafts. Shaping combinations depend on the fiction and table agreement; this composer does not decide every compatibility case.
            • Names and incantations use localized Glyph metadata, including alternate spellings.
